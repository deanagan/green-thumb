## Legend
**Tier 1 – Excellent florist rose**
- Long, strong stems  
- Holds form in heat  
- Excellent vase life  
- Handles deep, frequent cutting  

**Tier 2 – Very good garden cutter**
- Good stems, slightly shorter or softer  
- Best cut early  
- Excellent for home bouquets  

**Tier 3 – Garden-first, occasional cuts**
- Shorter stems or fast-opening blooms  
- Best for short vases or bowls  
- Grown mainly for garden display  


## Shade / Heat Protection – How to Read This Column

This column describes how much protection a rose benefits from during hot summers, especially in 35–45 °C heat and west-facing or heat-reflective locations.

### No (Loves sun)
- Thrives in full sun all day  
- Handles reflected heat well  
- Best for open beds or north/east aspects  
- Still benefits from mulch and deep watering in extreme heat  

**Typical placement:** Full sun

### Yes (Tolerant)
- Prefers full sun but copes well with heat  
- Can handle light afternoon shade without reducing flowering  
- Reliable choice for warm climates  

**Typical placement:** Full sun or light shade after ~3–4 pm

### Yes (Partial)
- Performs best with morning sun and afternoon shade  
- Flowers last longer and foliage looks better with protection  
- Strongly recommended for west-facing gardens or paving  

**Typical placement:** Sun until ~1–2 pm, then shade

### Yes (Afternoon)
- Actively benefits from shade during the hottest part of the day  
- Reduces petal scorch and stress in extreme heat  
- Very suitable for pots near walls or narrow side paths  

**Typical placement:** Morning sun, shaded from ~12–1 pm onward

### Essential
- Requires protection from hot afternoon sun to perform well  
- Without shade, blooms scorch and growth weakens  
- Best in east-facing positions or dappled light  

**Typical placement:** Morning sun only or filtered light

### Notes
- Roses grown in pots behave as if they are one category more heat-sensitive  
- West-facing brick walls can increase heat by 5–8 °C  
- All roses benefit from mulch, deep watering, and airflow during heatwaves
