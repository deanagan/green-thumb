### 🥗 Shade Salad Core
- Butterhead or loose-leaf lettuce
- Baby spinach
- Rocket (harvest young)
- Mizuna or tatsoi
- Spring onions (edges)

**Notes**
- Cut outer leaves weekly to keep height down
- Reseed small patches every 2–3 weeks

### 🌿 Upright Herb Pod
- Parsley (flat or curly)
- Coriander (cut early to prevent bolting)
- Chives
- Garlic chives
- Dill (centre position)

**Notes**
- Avoid basil, thyme, oregano
- Regular trimming keeps everything compact

### 🥬 Asian Greens Mix
- Baby pak choy
- Tatsoi
- Komatsuna
- Mustard greens
- Choy sum (harvest young)

**Notes**
- Plant densely
- Harvest at baby stage (20–25 cm)

### 🌱 Soup & Stir-Fry Base
- Spring onions
- Baby leeks
- Parsley
- Leaf celery
- Coriander

**Notes**
- Excellent cut-and-come-again plants
- Very cover-friendly

### 🍓 Strawberries + Greens
- Alpine strawberries (kept trimmed)
- Baby spinach or lettuce between plants

**Notes**
- Fruit will be smaller in low sun
- Plants stay healthy under the cover

# Vegepod Planting Guide  
**Alpine Strawberries + Spinach**  
*North-facing sun (long side facing north)*

---

## Plant Quantities
- 🍓 **Alpine strawberries:** 8 plants  
- 🥬 **Spinach (baby leaf):** 6–8 plants  

This balance maintains airflow under the cover and avoids crown rot.

---

### Legend
- **A** = Alpine strawberry  
- **S** = Spinach  

---

## Spacing Guidelines
- Alpine strawberries: **20–25 cm apart**
- Spinach: **15–20 cm apart**
- Keep spinach **5–7 cm clear of strawberry crowns**

---

## Vegepod-Specific Tips
- Trim strawberry leaves that grow inward or touch soil
- Harvest spinach early and often (cut-and-come-again)
- Avoid keeping soil constantly wet (humidity builds up under cover)
- Feed lightly every **2–3 weeks** (diluted liquid fertiliser or seaweed)

---

## Expected Results
- Healthy strawberry crowns
- Smaller but consistent alpine fruit in part sun
- Tender spinach leaves
- Reduced pest pressure under the cover
