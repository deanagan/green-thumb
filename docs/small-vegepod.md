### 🥗 Shade Salad Core
- Butterhead or loose-leaf lettuce
- Baby spinach
- Rocket (harvest young)
- Mizuna or tatsoi
- Spring onions (edges)

**Notes**
- Cut outer leaves weekly to keep height down
- Reseed small patches every 2–3 weeks

### 🌿 Upright Herb Pod
- Parsley (flat or curly)
- Coriander (cut early to prevent bolting)
- Chives
- Garlic chives
- Dill (centre position)

**Notes**
- Avoid basil, thyme, oregano
- Regular trimming keeps everything compact

### 🥬 Asian Greens Mix
- Baby pak choy
- Tatsoi
- Komatsuna
- Mustard greens
- Choy sum (harvest young)

**Notes**
- Plant densely
- Harvest at baby stage (20–25 cm)

### 🌱 Soup & Stir-Fry Base
- Spring onions
- Baby leeks
- Parsley
- Leaf celery
- Coriander

**Notes**
- Excellent cut-and-come-again plants
- Very cover-friendly

### 🍓 Strawberries + Greens
- Alpine strawberries (kept trimmed)
- Baby spinach or lettuce between plants

**Notes**
- Fruit will be smaller in low sun
- Plants stay healthy under the cover

### 🌸 Edible & Beneficial Mix
- Calendula
- Coriander (allow one plant to flower)
- Dill
- Lettuce filler

**Notes**
- Keeps pests down nearby
- Still fully cover-safe
