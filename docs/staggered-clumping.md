How to visualize / measure in your bed
	1.	Mother’s Love: mark base as point 0
	2.	Tiffany: measure 45 cm back (toward wall) and 40 cm left
	•	Draw an imaginary line from Mother’s Love → Tiffany
	•	This is at ≈ 45° from the front edge line
	3.	Dark Desire: from Tiffany, measure 35–40 cm back and 15–20 cm further left
	•	Angle ≈ 30° relative to Tiffany line toward left
	4.	Wall clearance: leave 30–35 cm between back plant (Dark Desire) and wall

⸻

Quick checks:
	•	Distance between Mother’s Love and Dark Desire: ~75 cm
	•	Distance between Tiffany and Dark Desire: ~35–40 cm
	•	Depth used: ~75 cm of 80 cm available → fits well
	•	Visual effect: diagonal line, staggered layers, full clump look

⸻

🌱 Tips for planting in this layout
	•	Use stakes or small markers to visualize angles before digging
	•	Mulch entire triangle area 5–8 cm thick
	•	Prune lightly in winter so all three grow to similar height
	•	Water as one unit for optimal root establishment
